Create one full-screen desktop browser application design mockup, landscape 16:9, for an interactive bird-flocking simulation called FLOCK / Earth in motion. This is a complete usable simulation workspace, not a marketing page, no browser chrome. Art direction: quiet scientific atlas meets elegant observatory, deep midnight navy background, fine slate borders, warm ivory clean sans-serif typography, restrained mint, amber and lavender accents. Crisp high-contrast readable UI, spacious but practical. The dominant central element is a large beautiful three-dimensional Earth sphere, about 65 percent of screen height, accurately recognizable continents in desaturated sage with midnight blue oceans, fine latitude and longitude grid, marked equator and polar circles, subtle ice caps, a realistic half-lit day/night terminator. Show several visually distinct flocks as small colored chevron birds just above the spherical surface with short curved trails hugging the globe, some amber, some mint, some lavender. No orbital satellite rings. Polar sunlight and the seasonal hemisphere difference should be visually understandable. Main globe sits on the left 72 percent of the interface with subtle depth and no distracting stars. Header has a tiny bird symbol, FLOCK wordmark, small subtitle Earth in motion, and a truthful status badge Illustrative simulation. Right sidebar roughly 300 pixels wide is a beautiful integrated control surface with heading Simulation. Include a date control reading Jun 21, a UTC hour slider, Playback with a Pause button and a speed selector, and a compact season section stating Northern summer / Southern winter. Include a Flocks section showing three color-coded groups named Northern migrants, Southern migrants, and Equatorial residents, with modest bird counts as illustrative design placeholders. Include three simple behavioral sliders labelled Separation, Alignment, Cohesion. Include a clearly visible Reset simulation button. At the globe bottom a slim floating bar reads Drag to rotate / Scroll to zoom, plus small accessible labelled Zoom in, Zoom out and Reset view buttons. Along the bottom of the globe area show a slim month timeline from Jan through Dec with Jun selected, and a small legend Daylight / Migration trails. The screen must feel like an actual buildable browser simulation, not a futuristic cockpit. Avoid decorative fake charts, business metrics, excessive glowing effects, paragraphs, fantasy landmasses, photography and unrelated navigation. All text should be cleanly set; this raster is visual direction, not final production copy.
